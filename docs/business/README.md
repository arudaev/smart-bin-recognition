# Business

Audited 2026-08-20. **Pre-customer, pre-pilot, no revenue.**

## Verdict

Smart Bin Recognition is a public product with a business hypothesis, not a
company with a proven market.

Germany already has mature municipal waste-app vendors, and ZAW Donau-Wald
already runs an Abfall+ app. AI item-to-bin guidance also exists. "AI + local
waste rules" is not a moat.

The narrower hypothesis worth testing is:

> A person standing at an unfamiliar bin can identify that physical container
> and retrieve the authority-backed local rule faster and more safely than with
> signage, search, or the existing operator app.

If that bin-first job is weak, the commercial case is weak. We do not quietly
pivot to recognising the item in the user's hand; that is a different product
and model.

## Business thesis

- The resident experience stays free, anonymous, multilingual, and accessible.
- The first proving ground is one managed student residence, not "a city."
- THD's International Office is a recruitment channel, not automatically the
  customer. The residence operator owns the site; ZAW owns the disposal rules.
- A first contract, if one exists, is for verified setup and an operated
  service—not for app downloads or scans.
- Scale has two possible routes: direct contracts with managed sites/operators,
  or integration into an incumbent municipal-app vendor. Neither is validated.
- Grants and prizes may finance evidence. They are not customer demand.

## Fixed boundaries

- No resident subscription, advertising, or sale of personal, location, or
  image data.
- No paywall around disposal guidance.
- No paid per-inference dependency in the common path.
- No unverified disposal rule and no commercial override of `unknown`.
- No analytics product that creates identities, journeys, or household-level
  surveillance.
- No monetary EVC, willingness-to-pay, contamination-reduction, or market-size
  claim without named evidence.

## Evidence language

Use **decision**, **observed**, **external**, **hypothesis**, and **unknown**.
Every number names its population, period, source, and method. Vendor-reported
reach is evidence of activity, not proof of impact.

## Next business decision

Do not choose a price or build an institutional dashboard. First determine
whether a managed residence and ZAW will support a comparative pilot of the
bin-first job. The required evidence is in the
[validation plan](05-validation-plan.md).
