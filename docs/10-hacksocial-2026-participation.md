# Hacksocial 2026 participation

Decision recorded: 11 August 2026.

## Status

Smart Bin Recognition will participate in **Hacksocial 2026** as a solo entry.
This document records the commitment and the published requirements. It does
not yet define the hackathon scope; the future Devpost playbook will do that.

- Event: [Hacksocial 2026](https://hacksocial2026.devpost.com/)
- Rules: [official rules](https://hacksocial2026.devpost.com/rules)
- Format: online
- Submission window: 1–31 August 2026
- Deadline: 31 August at 17:00 EDT, which is 1 September at 00:00 in Kyiv
- Intended track: AI/ML, subject to the later playbook

## Solo eligibility

**Confirmed.** The rules allow teams of **1–4 members**, so one person is a
valid team. The event is restricted to students aged 13 or older. Participation
therefore remains eligible while the maintainer is a current student at the
submission deadline and no standard territorial exclusion applies.

## Published expectations

The submission must provide, before the deadline:

- a project description explaining the problem;
- a way for judges to evaluate the project, such as a demonstration video,
  live link, or source repository;
- the repository or code files and a concise account of the inspiration,
  technology and learning, as requested on the event overview;
- original work that does not expose confidential or unlawfully collected
  material; and
- compliance with third-party and open-source licences.

The published judging criteria are technical execution, innovation and
creativity, and user-interface/design quality. The organizer may also consider
impact and completeness under the rules.

The published rules do not forbid a pre-existing project. The submission must
nevertheless identify the pre-event baseline and state plainly which work was
completed during Hacksocial.

## Repository note

The entrant retains the project's IP. Submission grants the organizers a
non-exclusive promotional licence over the project name, description,
screenshots and submission media; it does not grant rights to underlying source
beyond what is made public by the entrant.

Registration, final track selection, rescoping, demo design and submission
copy remain pending human action and the dedicated Devpost playbook.
